# TRIQ Vision

Date: 2026-11-06. Source: the founder's master blueprint ("TRIQ — The Complete Sports Ecosystem"), adopted 2026-11-06 (see `TRIQ_DECISIONS.md`). Current build status lives in `TRIQ_FEATURE_MATRIX.md` and `TRIQ_AUDIT.md`, not here.

## Mission

Build the digital infrastructure that connects every participant, organisation, service and opportunity in sports.

Positioning: **the operating system for sports.** TRIQ is not an academy app with extra modules. Academy OS is where real use is today, and it is the first journey to finish.

## Central philosophy

> Every athlete has a journey. Every organisation has an operating requirement. Every sports professional has an opportunity. TRIQ connects all three.

The verticals must work together. An athlete should experience one connected platform, not many separate apps. We finish connected journeys before we add screens.

## Principles

### 1. Athlete first

The athlete is the centre of the product. Data about an athlete serves the athlete's development, not the convenience of the academy, sponsor or selector.

- The athlete (or guardian, for a minor) owns their passport and controls what is public.
- Self-reported and independently verified information are always marked apart.
- Athletes are not compared across age groups, disciplines or testing protocols without valid context.
- No rule punishes an athlete for being sick, injured or absent. Points are never deducted for missed sessions.

### 2. Wellbeing is not a performance score

Psychological wellbeing is not something to rank, reward or sell.

- Wellbeing and clinical data are kept apart from coaching data (three tiers, see `TRIQ_DATA_PRIVACY.md`).
- Nothing about wellbeing is ranked, scored, rewarded, put on a leaderboard or shown to opponents, selectors or sponsors.
- Coaches and academy owners get no automatic access to private psychological records.
- TRIQ never pressures anyone to disclose private feelings. No streaks or badges for disclosure.
- We do not use metrics that reward hiding distress, such as "fewer distress reports" or "higher confidence scores".
- TRIQ teaches mental skills. Qualified professionals do clinical work. AI never diagnoses.

### 3. Safeguarding is non-negotiable

Most TRIQ athletes are minors. Age-appropriate experiences, guardian controls, consent, verified professionals, protected records, abuse reporting, moderation, data minimisation, retention, deletion and emergency protocols are platform requirements, not features.

TRIQ never sells access to confidential health information and never uses psychological data for advertising.

### 4. Real before wide

A vertical is released only when its workflows, permissions, safeguards, tests and customer validation are ready. Switching on verticals with no users is on the stop list (`TRIQ_ROADMAP.md`).

## What success looks like

A platform where an athlete can develop physically and mentally; a coach can manage and develop athletes; a psychologist, physiotherapist or nutritionist can provide professional support within proper boundaries; an academy, gym, club or venue can run its business; an organiser can run tournaments; a brand can support athletes; a creator can earn; a company can run sports programmes; a student can find sports education; and a community can play. All through one connected, secure platform.

Success metrics follow the blueprint (section 22). Wellbeing metrics are aggregate, voluntary and professionally governed only: programme participation, service access, appointment completion, follow-up engagement, education completion, referral responsiveness.
