TRIQ — Complete 11-Vertical Product Scope

Mandatory addition to the Master Product & Engineering Plan

1. Product mandate

TRIQ is the Operating System for Sports.

The product vision includes all 11 verticals listed below. Every vertical must be included in the master roadmap, product architecture, feature inventory, dependency map, and detailed specifications.

Implementation sequencing is not scope exclusion. Claude must plan all 11 verticals from the beginning, then implement them incrementally according to dependencies, risk, customer value, and technical readiness.

Do not reduce TRIQ to an academy-management SaaS product. Academy management is the initial commercial entry point into a broader sports ecosystem.

The product must ultimately connect athletes, coaches, academies, clubs, gyms, schools, universities, sports facilities, competitions, employers, wellness professionals, creators, sellers, sponsors, federations, and sports institutions.

Voice AI remains deferred. All verticals should be designed to support future integration with a shared assistant, without implementing voice features now.

2. Complete product verticals

Vertical 1 — TRIQ Business

Purpose: Operate sports academies, clubs, gyms, and training businesses.

Required feature groups

* Organization registration and onboarding.
* Organization profiles and branding.
* Branch and location management.
* Staff, memberships, invitations and role-based permissions.
* Student and athlete enrollment.
* Coach management and assignments.
* Batch creation and scheduling.
* Training sessions and attendance.
* Trial bookings and enrollment conversion.
* Fee plans, fee records, receipts and outstanding balances.
* Facility allocation.
* Operational calendar.
* Parent and student communications.
* Operational dashboard and business reports.
* Organization settings and audit history.

Required user journeys

1. Register an academy and configure its profile.
2. Invite staff and assign permissions.
3. Register a student and enroll them in a batch.
4. Assign a coach and training schedule.
5. Record attendance.
6. Assess a trial and convert it into enrollment.
7. Record and reconcile fee payments.
8. Review revenue, outstanding balances, attendance and utilization.
9. Manage staff and organization operations.

Completion requirement

A real academy must be able to manage its daily operations through persisted, secure, integrated workflows—not disconnected screens.

Vertical 2 — TRIQ Athlete

Purpose: Give athletes a persistent sporting identity and a pathway to development and opportunity.

Required feature groups

* Athlete identity and sporting profile.
* Sport, position, level and experience.
* Achievements and competition history.
* Training history and development goals.
* Coach feedback and progress reports.
* Skill and fitness assessments.
* Athlete portfolio and permitted media.
* Profile privacy and verification status.
* Trials, scholarships and recruitment opportunities.
* Applications and application history.
* Saved opportunities.
* Athlete development timeline.
* Guardian consent and age-appropriate access controls.

Required user journeys

1. Create an athlete profile.
2. Connect to an academy or authorized coach.
3. View training history and progress.
4. Receive and review coach assessments.
5. Maintain achievements and sporting history.
6. Discover relevant trials or scholarships.
7. Apply and track application status.
8. Share selected profile information with authorized organizations.

Completion requirement

Athletes can manage their sporting identity, understand their development, and discover opportunities without exposing private information or fabricating verified achievements.

Vertical 3 — TRIQ Play

Purpose: Power competitions and participation across the sports ecosystem.

Required feature groups

* Competition and tournament discovery.
* Competition creation and configuration.
* League and tournament formats.
* Team and individual registration.
* Eligibility rules.
* Registration fees where supported.
* Participant management.
* Fixtures and schedules.
* Match officials and assignments where supported.
* Score and result recording.
* Standings and leaderboards.
* Competition notifications.
* Withdrawals, cancellations and postponements.
* Historical results and competition profiles.
* Organizer analytics.

Required user journeys

1. Organizer creates a competition.
2. Organizer defines the format, dates, venue and eligibility.
3. Participants or teams register.
4. The system validates registration and capacity.
5. Fixtures are created according to supported rules.
6. Authorized officials record results.
7. Standings are calculated.
8. Participants view schedules and results.
9. Organizers review participation and operational performance.

Completion requirement

Competition creation, registration, scheduling, results and standings must follow explicit rules and remain consistent under concurrent updates.

Vertical 4 — TRIQ Spaces

Purpose: Make sports facilities discoverable, bookable and operationally manageable.

Required feature groups

* Venue and facility profiles.
* Sports and amenities.
* Location and discovery.
* Operating hours.
* Pricing and slot configuration.
* Availability calendars.
* Booking and reservation management.
* Booking confirmation and cancellation.
* Rescheduling and refund policies.
* Facility-owner dashboards.
* Utilization reports.
* Maintenance or unavailable-slot management.
* Venue reviews and ratings if moderation is implemented.
* Facility-level permissions.

Required user journeys

1. A venue operator creates a facility listing.
2. The operator configures availability and pricing.
3. A user searches for a suitable venue.
4. The user selects an available slot.
5. TRIQ validates availability and creates the booking.
6. The operator manages the booking.
7. Users can cancel or reschedule under the applicable policy.
8. Operators review utilization and booking performance.

Completion requirement

Availability must be accurate, concurrent bookings must not double-book resources, and financial states must not be fabricated.

Vertical 5 — TRIQ Careers & Professionals

Purpose: Connect sports professionals to jobs, organizations and career opportunities.

Required feature groups

* Professional profiles.
* Coaching and sports-industry job listings.
* Experience and qualifications.
* Skills and certifications.
* Employer and organization profiles.
* Job discovery and filtering.
* Job applications.
* Applicant management.
* Interview or recruitment status where supported.
* Saved jobs.
* Credential verification status.
* Career opportunities outside active competition.
* Career transition resources where supported.

Required user journeys

1. A professional creates a profile.
2. An employer creates a job listing.
3. Professionals discover and apply to relevant jobs.
4. Employers review and manage applications.
5. Application status changes are recorded.
6. Verified qualifications are displayed only when supported by a genuine verification process.

Completion requirement

Professionals and employers can manage a transparent recruitment workflow while protecting applications and personal information.

Vertical 6 — TRIQ Wellness

Purpose: Connect athletes and sports organizations to appropriate wellness professionals and services.

Required feature groups

* Provider profiles.
* Physiotherapy and rehabilitation services.
* Sports nutrition.
* Recovery services.
* Sports psychology services.
* Service discovery.
* Availability and appointments.
* Provider credentials and verification status.
* Booking history.
* Consent and access controls.
* Cancellation and rescheduling.
* Relevant payment workflow where supported.

Required user journeys

1. A provider creates a service profile.
2. A user discovers a relevant provider.
3. The user requests an appointment.
4. The provider confirms or manages availability.
5. The appointment lifecycle is recorded.
6. Authorized parties can view only the information necessary for their role.

Completion requirement

Health-related information must be protected as sensitive data. Public profiles and general organization dashboards must not expose private health records. Clinical decision-making AI is outside the initial scope.

Vertical 7 — TRIQ Creators

Purpose: Help sports creators, athletes and coaches build audiences and eventually monetize their work.

Required feature groups

* Creator profiles.
* Sports and content categories.
* Content portfolios.
* Content discovery.
* Links to relevant athlete, coach, venue or event profiles.
* Publishing and content management where supported.
* Engagement analytics based on actual events.
* Audience management where justified.
* Sponsorship and collaboration opportunities.
* Content reporting and moderation.
* Monetization capabilities as a later defined release.

Required user journeys

1. A creator creates a profile.
2. The creator publishes or links a portfolio.
3. Users discover relevant sports content.
4. Organizations identify potential collaborators.
5. Creators receive collaboration or sponsorship opportunities.
6. Analytics show only measured, trustworthy activity.

Completion requirement

Creators can establish a sports identity and discover commercial opportunities. Do not fabricate followers, views, engagement or earnings.

Vertical 8 — TRIQ Commerce

Purpose: Connect the sports ecosystem to equipment, apparel, products and services.

Required feature groups

* Seller and merchant profiles.
* Product and service listings.
* Categories and sport-specific discovery.
* Search and filters.
* Inventory and availability.
* Pricing and product details.
* Cart and checkout when implemented.
* Orders and order history.
* Seller order management.
* Cancellations and returns.
* Refund processing.
* Reviews and moderation where supported.
* Payment integration and reconciliation where supported.
* Seller settlements and commissions once the commercial model is defined.

Required user journeys

1. A seller creates a profile and listing.
2. A user discovers a product.
3. The user reviews availability, price and policies.
4. The user places an order through a real supported checkout.
5. The seller processes the order.
6. Order status is updated.
7. Cancellations, refunds and disputes follow defined policies.

Completion requirement

No simulated purchase should be presented as a real transaction. Inventory, payment, order and refund states must remain consistent.

Vertical 9 — TRIQ Brand Studio

Purpose: Connect brands, sponsors, athletes, academies, events and social-impact programs.

Required feature groups

* Brand and sponsor profiles.
* Sponsorship opportunity listings.
* Athlete and team sponsorship opportunities.
* Academy and event sponsorship.
* Campaign objectives and target audiences.
* Budgets and deliverables.
* Proposal submission and review.
* Scholarship and CSR programs.
* Campaign milestones.
* Deliverable tracking.
* Performance reporting where actual data exists.
* Agreement and approval workflows.

Required user journeys

1. A sponsor creates a campaign opportunity.
2. An eligible athlete, academy or organizer discovers it.
3. The participant submits a proposal.
4. The sponsor reviews and updates proposal status.
5. Approved parties agree on deliverables.
6. Campaign progress and completion are recorded.
7. Results are reported using verified data.

Completion requirement

Sponsorship proposals, agreements, approvals and deliverables must be traceable. Financial commitments must not occur without explicit authorization.

Vertical 10 — TRIQ Education

Purpose: Support sports participation and development in schools, colleges, universities and educational institutions.

Required feature groups

* Institution profiles.
* Sports departments and programs.
* Teams and squads.
* Coaches and staff.
* Training schedules.
* Inter-school and inter-university events.
* Student participation records.
* Competition results.
* Scholarships and applications.
* Sports internships.
* Institution-level reports.
* Guardian permissions and age-appropriate access.

Required user journeys

1. An institution establishes its sports profile.
2. Authorized staff create programs and teams.
3. Students join approved activities.
4. Coaches manage schedules and participation.
5. Institutions organize events.
6. Students discover relevant scholarships or internships.
7. Administrators review participation and program outcomes.

Completion requirement

Institutional workflows must respect school and university permissions, protect minors and keep student information appropriately scoped.

Vertical 11 — TRIQ Enterprise

Purpose: Provide sports federations, institutional networks, large clubs and multi-location operators with higher-level operational capabilities.

Required feature groups

* Multi-branch and multi-location administration.
* Organization hierarchy.
* Consolidated reporting.
* Role and permission administration.
* Federation and institutional workflows.
* Competition administration across eligible organizations.
* Audit trails.
* Data exports.
* API and webhook management.
* Integration configuration.
* Usage and subscription management when defined.
* Advanced operational analytics.
* Organization-specific configuration.
* Enterprise support and administration.

Required user journeys

1. An enterprise organization is configured.
2. Authorized administrators establish the organization hierarchy.
3. Branches and relevant users are associated with the hierarchy.
4. Local users operate within their permitted branch.
5. Authorized leadership views consolidated reports.
6. Changes are audited.
7. External integrations exchange validated, permission-scoped data.

Completion requirement

Enterprise users can manage multiple organizations or branches without weakening tenant isolation. Consolidated access must be explicitly authorized and auditable.

3. Shared platform layer — required for all verticals

Claude must design shared capabilities once and reuse them across the product.

Identity and permissions

* Shared account and identity model.
* Organization membership.
* Role and permission checks.
* Active organization context.
* Account recovery and session management.
* Guardian relationships where applicable.
* Public versus private profile visibility.

Scheduling and availability

* Consistent time and timezone handling.
* Calendar and event infrastructure.
* Booking and schedule conflict validation.
* Cancellation and rescheduling rules.
* Notifications for schedule changes.

Payments and financial infrastructure

* Payment records.
* Provider transaction references where applicable.
* Idempotency.
* Refund and reconciliation workflows.
* Financial audit history.
* Currency and monetary precision.
* Permission-scoped financial reporting.

Each vertical must retain its own domain-specific business rules.

Search and discovery

* Search, filters and pagination.
* Visibility rules.
* Sport and location filters.
* Relevance and sorting.
* Performance safeguards.

Notifications

* In-app and email notifications as configured.
* Delivery status.
* Retry and duplicate prevention.
* User preferences.
* Organization-specific communication permissions.

Verification and trust

* Identity and credential verification states.
* Moderation and reporting.
* Audit trails.
* Abuse prevention.
* Appropriate guardian consent.
* Explicit distinction between submitted, reviewed and verified information.

Analytics and reporting

* Shared date and timezone conventions.
* Documented metric definitions.
* Access-controlled reports.
* Export permissions.
* Actual-data-only reporting.
* Auditability for sensitive exports.

Integration foundation

* Versioned API contracts where required.
* Webhooks with signature validation and retry protection.
* External integration credentials stored securely.
* Integration logs that do not expose secrets.
* Provider-independent interfaces where they offer practical value.

Future AI foundation

* Clean server-side business functions.
* Explicit tool permissions.
* Structured validation.
* Audit logging.
* Human approval for consequential actions.

Do not implement voice AI now.

5. Implementation policy

All 11 verticals must remain in the committed product roadmap.

However, Claude must deliver them through controlled increments:

1. Audit the current repository.
2. Map every vertical to existing code and identify missing functionality.
3. Write all 11 vertical specifications and the shared-platform specification.
4. Build or stabilize the shared foundation.
5. Complete TRIQ Business as the first commercial release.
6. Implement Athlete, Play and Spaces on the shared foundation.
7. Deliver Careers, Wellness, Creators, Commerce, Brand Studio and Education.
8. Deliver Enterprise capabilities as the shared platform and multi-organization workflows mature.
9. Validate cross-vertical journeys, tenant isolation, data integrity and performance.
10. Keep voice AI deferred until the core platform is stable.

The exact ordering of later modules can change when dependencies or customer evidence justify it. The scope of the 11 verticals must not silently disappear from the roadmap.

6. Definition of done

A vertical is not complete because its route or sidebar entry exists.

It is complete only when its defined workflows are functional, persist real data, enforce authorization, integrate with relevant shared services, handle errors, have appropriate tests and satisfy the documented acceptance criteria.

For every release, record:

* Implemented features.
* Remaining features.
* Tests executed and their results.
* Known defects.
* Database migration status.
* Commit and branch, if created.
* Preview and production deployment status, if verified.
* Outstanding risks and approvals.

Never claim a commit, migration or deployment occurred unless it has been confirmed.

7. Immediate instruction

Read this specification and the existing TRIQ master plan.

First inspect the repository and build the feature inventory. Then write all 11 vertical specifications and the shared-platform specification, mapping each requirement to existing files and identifying what is complete, partial, mocked, broken or missing.

After that, implement the highest-priority verified gap in small, testable increments.

Do not rewrite the application, introduce voice AI, create fake data to fill screens, or perform unapproved production changes.

The objective is to build the complete TRIQ sports ecosystem—not just its first vertical.
