# TRIQ Competitive Landscape: 11 Verticals

*Prepared 26 Oct 2026. Desk research only, using public web pages. Company figures are **self-reported** unless noted, and some come from dated articles (year given). Where a price is not public, the report says so. Nothing here was invented. Where a source looked unreliable, I left the figure out.*

**What TRIQ offers today:** an academy SaaS that is free up to 30 students, then ₹25 per student per month with a ₹749 minimum. The founding offer is 50% off for 12 months. Its positioning line is "The operating system for sports".

---

## 0. Headline findings (read this first)

1. **KIBI Sports (Bengaluru) is already marketing almost exactly TRIQ's 11-vertical story.** It has an admin portal for academies, clubs, leagues and federations; tournaments with live scoring and streaming; verified athlete passports; venue booking; pro-services booking (coaching, physio); a brand sponsorship marketplace with an "AI matchmaker"; CSR/ESG; and a shop. It describes itself as a "unified cloud operating system" and claims 100+ institutions, 90,000+ verified athletes and 257+ brand partners. Its portal is **free until 31 Mar 2027 for founding partners**, and it includes a 48-hour roster migration. This is TRIQ's most direct strategic competitor, and both its pitch and its price undercut TRIQ.
2. **Price is not a wedge in Indian academy software. TRIQ is at the expensive end.**

| Students | TRIQ (₹25/student, ₹749 min)* | ArenaFlow (public tiers, +18% GST) | KIBI / Play A Sport / TutorPe |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | ₹0 | ₹449 (21–35 tier) | ₹0 |
| 50 | ₹1,250 | ₹599 | ₹0 |
| 100 | ₹2,500 | ₹1,149 | ₹0 |
| 200 | ₹5,000 | ₹2,238 | ₹0 |

*\*Assumes that once an academy passes 30 students, every student is billed. If only students above 30 are billed, TRIQ is ₹749 / ₹1,750 / ₹4,250 at 50 / 100 / 200 students. The pricing page should state which rule applies.* The 50% founding offer brings TRIQ close to ArenaFlow for 12 months. After that it costs about twice as much. Play A Sport says "100% free, no commission". TutorPe says "100% free" with unlimited students.
3. **India's demand-side apps own the consumer.** Playo claims 5M+ players across 50+ sports. Hudle claims 1.5M players, 2,000+ venues and 60+ cities, and raised a $2.5M Series A in July 2025. KheloMore claims about 500k users and 1,500+ coaches. CricHeroes reported about 16M users and roughly 20,000 tournaments in 2023. These apps already run venue-side partner apps. TRIQ cannot win a cold-start consumer marketplace against them in v1.
4. **TRIQ's realistic advantage is trust and data integrity built into academy operations.** That means payment-method provenance, staff-verified achievements, progress reports built from real attendance, guardian consent and "actual data only" analytics. Most Indian tools sell fee collection plus WhatsApp. Few make verified records the product.

## 1. TRIQ Business (academy ops): Threat **HIGH**

| Competitor | One line | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **KIBI Sports** (IN) | "Unified cloud OS": rosters, multi-centre batches, recurring fees (UPI/card) with GST receipts, WhatsApp reminders, progress with injury flags, venues, tournaments | Free until 31 Mar 2027 (founding partners). Pricing after that not public |
| **ArenaFlow** (IN) | Multi-sport academy app: Razorpay/UPI, attendance by geofence or RFID, enquiry tracking, parent portal, GST invoices, free multi-location and white-label | ₹289/month (≤20 students) up to ₹4,500 (301–500). Add-ons: WhatsApp Pro ₹399, Tournaments ₹499–999, Coach Assessments ₹299, Coach Payroll ₹599, API ₹999 |
| **CoFee** (IN) | Fee-collection specialist: WhatsApp pay-links without an app, instalments, cash/cheque logging, GST invoices, branch reports | Not public |
| **Play A Sport** (IN) | Free academy app plus listing: batches, bookings, online fees, attendance, broadcasts, multi-branch | Free, no commission |
| **TutorPe** (IN) | Free fee app with sports templates: batches, partial payments, PDF receipts, bulk WhatsApp reminders, "Fee Intelligence AI" | Free |
| Global: **iClassPro**, **Upper Hand**, **Spond**, **TeamSnap** | Mature class and club SaaS (iClassPro: makeups and waitlists; Upper Hand: facility and packages; Spond: free team app with payments; TeamSnap: club registration) | iClassPro $139/$199/$299 per month. Upper Hand $79/$199 per month, Scale tier by quote. Spond free to use. TeamSnap free for single teams, clubs by quote |

**What they do better than TRIQ v1**
- **Price:** free or roughly half the cost (see table above).
- **Hardware and automation:** ArenaFlow offers RFID and geofence attendance. Several tools have a GST-compliant invoice flow built in. CoFee handles instalments and partial payments.
- **Migration:** KIBI does a 48-hour roster migration, and ArenaFlow gives the first month free to academies switching from another app.
- **Coach payroll and staff attendance** (ArenaFlow add-on; SalaryBox targets the same need). These matter because coaches are the academy's biggest cost.
- **Global tools:** makeup classes, waitlists, sibling and family billing, auto-pay mandates.

**Where TRIQ can win**
- **Payment-method provenance plus reconciliation.** Cash, UPI and Razorpay are tracked with an audit trail of who recorded what. Owners in India worry about cash leakage at branches, and most competitors only log that a payment happened.
- **One record from trial to enrolment to attendance to fees to progress.** Most rivals are either fee-first (CoFee, TutorPe) or listing-first (Play A Sport).
- **Honest reports with no fake demo data.** This is a credible message for multi-branch owners.

**Must-haves missing from the plan**
1. **GST-compliant invoicing plus UPI Autopay/e-mandate** for recurring fees, with partial payments and instalments. Competitors treat these as table stakes.
2. **Coach and staff attendance plus payroll** (per-session or per-batch pay).
3. **Assisted migration** (CSV/Excel import of students and fee balances, ideally done for the customer) and **makeup-class/waitlist handling.**

## 2. TRIQ Athlete (identity, progress, opportunities): Threat **MED**

| Competitor | One line | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **KIBI Sports** (IN) | Verified athlete profiles/passports, free mobile app, verified fundraising, sponsorship browsing | Athlete app free |
| **NSRS / Khelo India ID** (Govt of India) | National Sports Repository System: athlete and coach registration, a unique KID, achievements, performance assessment against federation parameters, coach-tagged trainees and attendance | Free (government) |
| **CricHeroes** (IN) | De facto cricket identity: career stats from scored matches, CricInsights analytics, highlights | Pro ₹99/month or ₹400/year (2023). Pro Infinity ₹3,400 |
| **SportVot** (IN) | Grassroots streaming plus athlete player cards and talent discovery | App free |
| **SportsApp** (IN) | Athlete network for sponsorship campaigns (100k+ athletes claimed; PUMA campaign) | Not public |

**What they do better**
- **Automatic stats from match scoring** (CricHeroes) and **video highlights** (CricHeroes, SportVot). TRIQ's identity is limited to academy data.
- **Government linkage.** NSRS has official federation assessment parameters and the KID, which talent-identification schemes use.

**Where TRIQ can win**
- **The monthly progress report parents actually read,** built from real attendance plus coach ratings and sent over WhatsApp. This drives retention for academies and makes the product sticky for parents.
- **Staff-verified achievements with a clear submitted, reviewed, verified distinction,** plus guardian-consented applications. KIBI also claims verification. TRIQ needs a visible, inspectable verification trail to stand apart.

**Must-haves missing**
1. **Shareable, parent-facing report and profile link** (PDF and web, with a consent toggle), sent on WhatsApp.
2. **Standardised fitness-test battery** aligned with Khelo India/NSRS-style parameters (sprint, agility, endurance) so results compare across academies. Optionally store the KID on the athlete profile.
3. **Media and highlights slot** (link YouTube or CricHeroes, upload short clips) under the moderation rules.

## 3. TRIQ Play (tournaments): Threat **HIGH for cricket, MED for other sports**

| Competitor | One line | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **CricHeroes** (IN) | Cricket scoring plus tournament management. About 16M users, about 20k tournaments, 240+ associations (2023) | Scoring free. Organiser value-added services. Streaming ₹199/match or ₹3,999/month (2023) |
| **KIBI Sports** (IN) | Registration, brackets, live scoring, streaming | Bundled (see above) |
| **SFA / SFAPlay** (IN) | Multi-sport school championships (31 sports) with a proprietary games management system, AI match video and fitness tests | Event entry fees. Software not sold |
| **Tournify** (NL) | Easy multi-sport tournament builder with a public app | Free ≤8 teams. €40 (≤60 teams) or €120 (unlimited) per tournament |
| **Challonge** (US) | Brackets: single/double elimination, round robin, Swiss, plus ticketing | Free tier. Paid about $7–12/month |

**What they do better**
- **Live ball-by-ball scoring and streaming** (CricHeroes, SportVot, KIBI). This is what makes grassroots tournaments go viral.
- **Double elimination, Swiss and group-to-knockout formats** (Challonge, Tournify). TRIQ plans only round-robin and knockout.
- **Online entry-fee collection.** ArenaFlow sells this as "Open Tournaments" at ₹999/month.

**Where TRIQ can win**
- **Academy-internal and inter-academy leagues.** TRIQ already holds the rosters, age groups and guardian consent, so eligibility checks (age, academy, batch) happen automatically and results go into the athlete profile. That is a closed loop competitors lack.
- Strong consistency under concurrent updates is a quality point, but it is not a selling point on its own.

**Must-haves missing**
1. **Group stage to knockout** and **double elimination**, plus seeding.
2. **Paid registration** via Razorpay with refunds on withdrawal.
3. **Basic live scoring** (point/goal/set level for badminton, football and pickleball) and a public results page. **Integrate with CricHeroes for cricket rather than rebuilding it.**

## 4. TRIQ Spaces (turf/court booking): Threat **HIGH**

| Competitor | One line | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Playo** (IN) | 5M+ players, 50+ sports. Consumer booking, GameTime matchmaking, coach discovery. A Venue Partner Interface app offers a scheduler, pricing engine, memberships and utilisation reports | Not public (commission-based per third-party sources; unverified) |
| **Hudle** (IN) | 1.5M players, 2,000+ venues, 60+ cities (Jul 2025). Hudle Partner app: slots, memberships, staff, GST invoicing, UPI, POS/equipment rental, white-label site, coupons, occupancy dashboard | Not public |
| **KheloMore** (IN) | Venue plus coach booking in Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru and Hyderabad, about 500k users | Not public |
| **Playtomic** (ES) | Padel/tennis club software plus player network, 5,500+ clubs in 52 countries (per a competitor's comparison page) | Not public |
| **CourtReserve** (US) | Court scheduling, member portal, website builder, player app | From $25/month (Capterra) |

**What they do better**
- **Built-in demand.** Playo and Hudle bring players to venues, which venue owners value more than software.
- **Dynamic and peak pricing, memberships, POS and equipment rental, GST invoicing, white-label sites** (Hudle Partner, Playo VPI). Matchmaking and player ratings (Hudle's GRIP rating, Playo GameTime).

**Where TRIQ can win**
- **Academy-owned facilities.** Many academies also rent their turf or courts after batch hours. One calendar that blocks academy batch slots *and* takes public bookings without double-booking is a real gap. Playo and Hudle do not run the academy side.
- **Venues that do not want to pay marketplace commission** for repeat or walk-in customers.

**Must-haves missing**
1. **Dynamic and peak/off-peak pricing plus memberships and packs** (e.g. 10-game pass).
2. **GST invoicing plus POS for rentals and add-ons.**
3. **Channel sync / iCal or partner integration with Playo and Hudle,** so a venue listed on those apps can still use TRIQ as its system of record. Without this, venues will not switch.

## 5. TRIQ Careers (coach profiles, jobs): Threat **LOW–MED**

| Competitor | One line | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn / Indeed India** | Default for corporate sports roles. Indeed lists sports jobs in India | Job posting is pay-per-post or promoted (varies) |
| **Federation coach registries** (NSRS, AIFF coach registration, Hockey India member-unit portal) | Official coach registration and licensing records | Free (official) |
| **TeamWork Online** (US) | Sports and live-events recruiting system licensed by teams. 100k+ hires claimed | Employer licence, not public |
| **WorkInSports** (US) | Sports job board | From about $316–$799/month for employers (Betterteam) |

**What they do better:** reach (LinkedIn) and **authoritative licence data** (federations hold the source of truth for AIFF and Hockey India coach licences).

**Where TRIQ can win:** no Indian incumbent focuses on **grassroots coach hiring by academies** (part-time, per-batch, city-specific). TRIQ also sees a coach's real batches and attendance history, which is a verifiable track record that LinkedIn cannot show.

**Must-haves missing**
1. **Verification tied to federation registries** (licence number plus document check, with an expiry date), not self-declared certificates.
2. **Background-check and child-safeguarding status** (police verification upload, safeguarding training). Indian academies will ask for this.
3. **Part-time, per-session gigs** (substitute coach for a batch), not only full-time listings.

## 6. TRIQ Wellness (physio, nutrition, psychology): Threat **MED (low direct competition, high execution risk)**

| Competitor | One line | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Practo** (IN) | Doctor and physio discovery and booking. The Bengaluru sports-physio listing shows fees of about ₹500–₹1,900 | Booking free to patients |
| **Physitrack** (global, India pricing) | Clinic software: home exercise programmes, video, PROMs | "1100r" (likely ₹1,100) per clinician per month per its page |
| **KIBI Sports** | Pro-services booking (coaching, physio, consultation) | Bundled |
| **Physiqcian / SmartPT** (IN) | Online physio consults | Not public |

**What they do better:** provider supply and SEO (Practo). Clinical tooling such as exercise libraries and outcome measures (Physitrack).

**Where TRIQ can win:** **referrals inside the academy.** A coach flags an injury, the athlete is referred to a partner physio, the guardian consents, and the physio sends back a return-to-play clearance the coach can see, with no clinical details exposed. Nobody does that loop for Indian academies.

**Must-haves missing**
1. **Injury log, return-to-play status and consent** (coach-visible status only), with **DPDP Act 2023 compliance** for health data, which counts as sensitive in practice.
2. **Integration with or referral to Practo or clinic software** rather than building a provider marketplace.

## Overall

### TRIQ's real wedge
**"The academy OS where the data is real":** trustworthy money, attendance and progress records for Indian multi-sport academies (especially multi-branch ones in NCR). Owners trust the collections numbers (provenance and audit), parents trust the monthly progress report (built from real attendance and coach ratings), and every later vertical (athlete profile, scholarships, CSR, school partnerships) inherits that verified data. TRIQ cannot win on price (free and cheaper rivals exist) or consumer reach (Playo and Hudle). It can win on integrity plus a completed workflow loop. Its positioning against KIBI should be **depth and verifiability in academy ops**, not a wider list of verticals.

### Five highest-impact feature additions
1. **UPI Autopay/e-mandate plus GST invoicing plus partial payments and instalments** (Business). This is table stakes, it directly lifts collections, and without it TRIQ loses evaluations.
2. **WhatsApp-delivered monthly progress report and shareable athlete card** (Athlete plus Business). Parents see value, retention goes up, and it spreads by word of mouth.
3. **Coach/staff attendance plus payroll** (Business, feeding into Careers). It answers the owner's biggest cost question, and it creates verified coach track records for Careers.
4. **Academy-plus-public turf booking on one conflict-free calendar, with peak pricing and memberships** (Spaces). This monetises idle academy slots and is a gap Playo and Hudle don't cover.
5. **CSR scholarship and fee-waiver programme with verified impact reports** (Brand Studio plus Athlete). This is a revenue source that uses TRIQ's "actual data" strength and is hard for fee-only tools to copy.

Also important: **assisted migration** (KIBI offers 48 hours, ArenaFlow a free first month) and **clarifying or rethinking pricing**. TRIQ is about twice ArenaFlow's price once the founding discount ends, while KIBI is free until March 2027.

### Where TRIQ should NOT compete head-on (partner or integrate instead)
| Area | Don't build | Instead |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer venue marketplace (Spaces) | Player-side discovery against Playo, Hudle and KheloMore | Be the venue/academy system of record. Pursue listing sync or partnership with Playo and Hudle |
| Cricket scoring and streaming (Play) | Ball-by-ball scoring and streaming | Link CricHeroes match and player IDs into athlete profiles |
| Creators | Native publishing and audience tools | YouTube/Instagram embeds plus an academy-to-creator gig board |
| Wellness marketplace | Provider supply and clinical tooling | Referral loop with partner physios, Practo links, return-to-play status only |
| Commerce logistics | Warehousing and delivery | Kit as an invoice line item plus a print-on-demand or local manufacturer partner |
| Federation registration (Enterprise) | Competing with NSRS and federation portals | Store the KID and federation licence numbers, and export in their formats |
| Sponsorship intelligence | A SponsorUnited-style data business | Focus on CSR and local academy/event sponsorship with verified delivery |

### Caveats
- **Unverified figures.** Playo's commission and partner pricing, Hudle's partner pricing, KheloMore's venue pricing, Playtomic's fees, Glofox's price (third-party median about $4,200/year, not official) and SOCS pricing are **not public**. Playtomic's club count comes from a competitor's comparison page. User and scale numbers are company claims. CricHeroes figures date from 2023.
- **Not found.** No company called "Spoyt" turned up in searches. The closest Indian match was Spolto (Pune; 1,200+ coaches and academies; $1M seed, 2024), which is a participation and booking platform. Sportz Village/EduSports scale could not be verified because the source page failed to load.
